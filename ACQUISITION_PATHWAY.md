# SSB Observer — Pathway to Acquisition

## Phase 1: Proof (0–18 months)

**Goal:** One paying customer. Real BBU data. Case study published.

### Month 0–3: Pilot Hunt

| Action | Success Metric |
|---|---|
| Contact 10 African tower operators via LinkedIn/email/introductions | 3-5 responses |
| Offer free 3-month pilot: "Give us anonymized BBU phase logs. We'll tell you which towers have structural debt. No cost." | 1-2 pilots started |
| Target: IHS Towers Kenya, ATC Ghana, Helios Towers, Eaton Towers | First data received |

**Why this works:** Free. No hardware. No tower climb. No risk to them. They already have the data. They're curious.

### Month 3–6: First Real Validation

| Action | Success Metric |
|---|---|
| Run observer on real BBU logs from 50-200 towers | Results delivered |
| Compare to their maintenance records: "Tower #47 was repaired in March — our observer shows debt accumulating in February" | 1+ correlation found |
| Write case study: "3 towers flagged for inspection. 2 confirmed structural issues. 1 false positive." | Case study published |

**The make-or-break moment:** Can we find a real tower that was repaired, and show our observer would have caught it earlier? One match is all we need.

### Month 6–12: First Revenue

| Action | Success Metric |
|---|---|
| Convert pilot to paid Alert Feed ($10/tower/month) | 50-200 towers paying |
| Integrate with their existing OSS/dashboard | Reference implementation |
| Present at industry conference (AfricaCom, TowerXchange) | Speaking slot |

### Month 12–18: Reference Customer

| Action | Success Metric |
|---|---|
| One named reference: "IHS Towers monitors X towers with SSB Observer" | Public case study |
| $12K-24K ARR | First revenue milestone |
| Second pilot with different operator | Pipeline growing |

## Phase 2: Product (18–36 months)

**Goal:** $500K-1M ARR. Multi-tower dashboar. Drone company partnership.

### Month 18–24: Integration Partnerships

| Action | Success Metric |
|---|---|
| Partner with 1-2 drone inspection companies: we provide the alert feed, they provide the visual inspection on demand | Signed partnership |
| Offer bundled pricing: "SSB Observer + drone inspection = $30/tower/month" | First bundled customer |
| Build API: `POST /alerts → drone flight schedule` | API live |

**Why this is the tipping point:** Drone companies have existing customers. We ride their distribution. They get a differentiator ("continuous monitoring + visual inspection"). We get customers without building a sales team.

### Month 24–30: Scale to 1,000+ Towers

| $120K-240K ARR | Revenue milestone |

### Month 30–36: Multi-Tower Dashboard

## Phase 3: Scale (36–60 months)

**Goal:** $3-5M ARR. Market leader in Africa. Acquisition offers incoming.

### Year 3–4: Continental Expansion

| Action | Success Metric |
|---|---|
| Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, Tanzania — replicate Kenya model | 5+ countries |
| 15,000-20,000 towers under contract | $2-3M ARR |
| Team of 8-12 (engineering + customer success + 1 sales) | Operating profit positive |

### Year 4–5: Regulatory Recognition

| Action | Success Metric |
|---|---|
| Work with African telecom regulators to recognize continuous BBU-based monitoring as equivalent to visual inspection | Standard adopted |
| This unlocks the entire market — every regulated operator must monitor towers. If our method is the standard, we win the continent. | Regulatory moat |

### Year 5: First Acquisition Offers

At $3-5M ARR, 20,000+ towers, regulatory recognition:
- Drone companies offer $5-15M
- IHS/ATC explore acquisition $10-30M
- SHM platform vendors explore $20-50M

**Do you sell at Year 5?** Depends on growth rate. If you're adding 500 towers/month and expanding globally, no — keep growing. If growth is plateauing at 2-3% monthly, yes.

## Phase 4: Exit (Year 5–7)

### Scenario A: Drone Company Acquisition (Most Likely)

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Acquirer | Pointivo, Optelos, Voliro |
| Rationale | Bundle: continuous + visual = complete tower monitoring |
| Price | $10-20M |
| Terms | Cash + earnout (you stay 2 years) |
| You walk away with | $5-10M (after earnout, dilution) |

**Why this path:** Drone companies need differentiation. Everyone has drones. Nobody has zero-hardware continuous monitoring. You're their missing piece. They have customers. You have the tech. Deal closes in 3 months, not 18.

### Scenario B: SHM Platform Acquisition (Highest Value)

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Acquirer | Bentley Systems, Hexagon, Siemens |
| Rationale | Telecom vertical entry. "We monitor bridges, dams, buildings — now add 1.2M towers." |
| Price | $30-80M (5-10× ARR) |
| Terms | Mostly cash. You're a product line, not the CEO. |
| You walk away with | $15-40M |

**Why this path:** Highest valuation. But requires significant ARR ($5M+) and regulatory adoption. Longest timeline.

### Scenario C: Towerco Strategic (Most Synergistic)

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Acquirer | IHS Towers, ATC |
| Rationale | Internal tool becomes competitive advantage. "Our towers cost 50% less to maintain than competitors'." |
| Price | $15-50M |
| Terms | Acquihire. Tech + team absorbed. You run the monitoring division. |
| You walk away with | $5-20M |

**Why this path:** You'd already be working inside IHS from the pilot phase. Natural progression: contractor → department → acquisition.

## The One Thing That Determines Everything

**Can we find one real tower where our observer would have caught a problem before it was found by existing methods?**

One case study. One tower. One "would have caught it 3 months earlier."

Everything else — the features, the metrics, the whitepaper, the dashboar — is scaffolding for that one piece of evidence. Once it exists, the path from pilot to acquisition is clear. Without it, nothing happens.

The entire company thesis reduces to: **get one real tower's BBU data, run the observer, find a match.**
