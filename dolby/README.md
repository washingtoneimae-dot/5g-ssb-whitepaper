# Dolby Patent Research

Dolby Laboratories (NYSE: DLB) generates **93% of revenue from patent licensing**.
With ~28,400 issued patents and 6,100 pending, they are one of the most aggressive
patent enforcers in audio/video technology. They also own Via LA, a major patent
pool administrator.

**Key targets of interest:** Subversion of royalty-free codec promises.
- [AV1](AV1.md) — Dolby bought GE's video compression patents ($429M, 2024) and
  is now suing AV1 implementers (Snap, TPV/Philips, Skyworth) for a codec
  that was promised royalty-free
- [Opus](OPUS.md) — Dolby + Fraunhofer created the Vectis IP pool demanding
  €0.15/unit for an IETF royalty-free audio codec
